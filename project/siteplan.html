Site name: Techup

A modern IT and software services company with a physical tech store.

Site Purpose

Techup provides IT and software solutions to individuals and businesses. The site also highlights our physical store, offering tech products for delivery or in-store pickup.

Scenarios

  • How can I hire Techup for software development?
  • What products are available in the Techup store and how can I buy them?

Color Scheme

Our site will use the following colors:

  • Primary Blue (#1E90FF) - headings, buttons, accents
  • White (#FFFFFF) - background, clean sections
  • Darker Blue (#0A74DA) - links, hover effects

Typography

  • Headings: Roboto Bold (modern, sans-serif)
  • Body Text: Open Sans Regular (clean sans-serif)
  • Optional/Tech Sections: Source Code Pro (monospace for code snippets or technical specs)

Pages

  • Home - Welcome message, overview of store and services
  • About - Detailed info about Techup’s services and team
  • Contact - Form to reach Techup, store location, and hours
